=== H01 | Hook | stage:hook | bg:wedding_ring ===
"Love is measured in dollars, dear." My future mother-in-law slid a list across the table. Bride's family contribution: fifty thousand dollars. And my father, a small-town carpenter, was left standing. Nobody offered him a chair.

=== H02 | The Spoons | stage:goguma | bg:wood_workshop ===
Dad had spent three months of evenings carving walnut spoons with our names on them. She picked one up with two fingers and laughed. "Wooden spoons? If you can't manage this much, maybe this marriage isn't a good idea." The spoon hit the floor, and my father got down on his knee in his only suit to pick it up.

=== H02B | Silence | stage:goguma | bg:dark_window_light ===
My fiancé stared at his water glass. Then she made Dad pay half of a nine hundred dollar dinner he never ate. On the drive home he whispered, "I'm sorry your dad isn't more." That night, he sanded those spoons again until two in the morning.

=== H03 | Second Chance | stage:goguma | bg:morning_light ===
Here's the thing. I was living that night for the second time. In my first life, she secretly used my bakery as collateral for a two hundred thousand dollar loan, and my father collapsed when the collectors came. Then I woke up a year earlier. This time, I hit record.

=== H03B | The Pawn Shop | stage:goguma | bg:rain_window ===
She mocked his workshop. She cut his name from the invitations. And one rainy afternoon, I found Dad outside a pawn shop, holding my late mother's wedding ring. "It's all I have to give you, Gracie." I held him on the sidewalk and made a promise.

=== H03C | The Broker | stage:goguma | bg:bakery_sunlight ===
Then a man in a leather jacket and a gold watch walked into my bakery and looked around like he was pricing everything. "Families help each other, friend. When they don't, life gets complicated." My phone, in my apron pocket, recorded every word.

=== H04 | The Papers | stage:turning | bg:paper_document ===
Two weeks before the wedding, she pushed a mortgage on my building across the table. "Do not tell Richard. Sign, or forget the wedding." That night she texted me: "Who does a carpenter think he is?" So I called the one man who had been quietly watching. My father-in-law.

=== H05 | The Chair | stage:saida | bg:bright_living_room ===
At the family dinner, she spread the list out again. Again, no chair for Dad. Then Richard walked out of his study, carried a chair over himself, and sat my father down. "Put that away, Diane. Before you embarrass yourself any further."

=== H06 | Justice | stage:saida | bg:sunlight_hall ===
The lawyer laid out the loan, the mortgage, the recordings. The so-called broker was arrested at the airport. Her lake house and jewelry went to pay her debt. And Richard said, "My father was a carpenter too." She got down on her knees and apologized to my dad.
